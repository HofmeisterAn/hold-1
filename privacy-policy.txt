Privacy Policy for Hold: Train Healthier

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Hold ("the app") is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not run
servers, we do not operate analytics, and we do not collect, sell, or share
your personal information. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

Summary

- No data leaves your device to us or any third party.
- No accounts, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics.
- Health and workout data are used only on your device to show your heart-rate
  zones.

Information the app accesses

Apple Health (read-only). With your permission, Hold reads your date of birth,
biological sex, and resting heart rate to calculate your maximum heart rate and
personalized training zones. This data is read on demand, used only to perform
that calculation on your device, and is never written back to Health, stored on
a server, or transmitted to us. You can grant or revoke this access at any time
in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Health.

Bluetooth. Hold connects to your heart-rate sensor over Bluetooth to read your
live heart rate during workouts. The name and identifier of sensors you pair
are stored locally on your device so the app can reconnect to them. This
information is not transmitted anywhere.

Workout sessions. Heart-rate samples and workout summaries you record are stored
locally on your device. They remain on your device and are included in your
device backups if you have those enabled (for example, iCloud or encrypted
local backups managed by Apple); we never receive them.

App settings. Preferences such as your chosen theme are stored locally on your
device.

Purchases

Hold offers optional in-app tips to support development. Purchases are
processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name,
payment details, or any other personal information from these transactions -
only an anonymous confirmation that a purchase completed. Apple's handling of
payment information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Data sharing

We do not share your information with any third party, because we never collect
it in the first place. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising,
or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data retention and deletion

All data the app creates or accesses stays on your device. To delete it,
uninstall the app. To revoke Health or Bluetooth permissions without
uninstalling, use the iOS Settings app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
location with a revised "Last updated" date.
